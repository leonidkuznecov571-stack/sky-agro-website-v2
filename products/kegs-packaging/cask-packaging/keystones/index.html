Keystones

For standard UK ale casks

Durable plastic keystones for the keystone hole on standard UK casks, built to withstand routine filling, transport and cellar tapping. Designed for dependable seal integrity before service and a clean drive when tapped in the trade.

Keystones are a core consumable for cask filling and dispense, fitted into the keystone hole at the face of the cask and designed to hold securely until the cask is tapped for service. A dependable keystone helps reduce leakage during handling while still allowing consistent tapping performance in the pub cellar.

These keystones are suited to routine brewery production, wholesaler handling and trade dispense. They are intended for single use and should be fitted to clean, undamaged casks using standard cask preparation practice.
