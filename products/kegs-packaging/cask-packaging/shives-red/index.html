Shives (Red)

Colour: Red

Standard red plastic shives for sealing the shive hole on 9-gallon (firkin) and 18-gallon (kilderkin) casks. Manufactured from food-grade polyethylene, they provide a reliable pressure seal during secondary conditioning and cellar storage, and are colour-coded red for straightforward identification on the racking line.

Red shives are a standard consumable for ale and cask beer producers. Fitted into the shive hole on top of the cask immediately after racking, they provide a sealed environment for secondary fermentation and conditioning while the bung membrane absorbs CO₂ pressure. The red colour coding is the UK industry standard for full or new casks on the racking line, helping to differentiate from conditioned or returned casks using clear or differently coloured fittings.

The shive is designed to be driven home with a mallet and forms a tight interference fit with standard cask bung holes. The pre-scored centre membrane is punctured with a vent peg or hard spile during conditioning, and the hole is subsequently sealed with a soft spile until the beer is ready to serve.

Compatible with all standard 9-gallon (firkin) and 18-gallon (kilderkin) casks in the UK trade. Contact us to confirm compatibility with pin or barrel formats.
