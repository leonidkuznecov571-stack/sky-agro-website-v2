30L keg with bag

Capacity: 30 litres

30L PET keg with sterile inner bag for oxygen-free dispensing, extended shelf life, and consistent beer quality across distribution.

A 30L PET keg with an integrated aseptic inner bag system, designed to separate beer from propellant gas during dispensing. Compressed air or CO2 is applied between the outer shell and inner bag, ensuring the product is pushed out without gas contact, preserving integrity throughout the dispense cycle.

Ideal for breweries requiring extended distribution range and product stability, this format reduces oxidation risk, improves shelf life, and eliminates the need for complex gas management. It also lowers cleaning and return logistics, making it a cost-efficient solution for export and contract supply.
