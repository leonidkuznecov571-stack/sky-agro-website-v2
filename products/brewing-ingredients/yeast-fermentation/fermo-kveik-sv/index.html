Fermo Kveik SV is a Norwegian-origin top-fermenting yeast selected for high-temperature fermentation and fast turnaround. It produces a fruity profile with notes of orange peel and citrus, alongside medium to high ester expression and low sulphur character.

Its high temperature tolerance and fast fermentation cycle support reduced tank residency times and improved brewery throughput. It is particularly well suited to farmhouse ales, IPAs, and hazy styles, as well as production environments where speed, robustness, and consistency are critical.
