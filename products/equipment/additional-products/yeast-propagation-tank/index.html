Yeast management

Yeast Propagation Tank

Working volume: 250 L with 100% headspace

The Yeast Propagation Tank is a dedicated vessel for controlled yeast growth, designed to maintain sterile conditions during propagation and storage prior to pitching. It is typically used as part of a propagation system or as a standalone yeast management vessel.

Manufactured in food-grade stainless steel, the tank includes aeration, temperature control, and sanitary fittings to support yeast health and viability across propagation cycles.
