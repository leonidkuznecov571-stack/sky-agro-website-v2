Yeast Propagation Tank

Working volume: 250 L with 100% headspace

The Yeast Propagation Tank is a stainless steel pressure vessel for controlled yeast growth, recovery, and pitching in craft breweries and distilleries. With integrated CIP/SIP, sterile aeration, glycol temperature control, polished internal finish, and optional automation, it helps brewers maintain healthy yeast, improve fermentation reliability, and reduce recurring yeast costs.
