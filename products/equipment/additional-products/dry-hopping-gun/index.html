Dry Hopping Gun

Efficient hop oil and aroma extraction

The Dry Hopping Gun is a sanitary hop infusion system designed to extract oils and aromas from hop pellets efficiently and consistently. By circulating beer through the hop charge, it improves contact between beer and hops, supports a more homogeneous infusion, reduces hop wastage, and helps brewers achieve stable aroma expression with safer operation.
