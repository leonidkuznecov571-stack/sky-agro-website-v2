Tubular Conveyor

The Tubular Conveyor is a flexible conveying system for transporting malt and other dry materials within a brewery. It is designed to move product efficiently between storage, milling, and brewhouse stages while minimising dust and product loss.

Using enclosed piping and mechanical conveying elements, the system supports horizontal and vertical transport across complex layouts.
