Yeast management

Yeast Propagation System

The Yeast Propagation System is a controlled process unit for expanding yeast cell counts under sterile conditions prior to pitching. It enables breweries to maintain yeast health, consistency, and availability without reliance on external supply or repeated repitching cycles.

The system integrates sterile aeration, temperature control, agitation, and staged propagation vessels to support stepwise yeast growth. Designed for both craft and larger-scale breweries, it ensures consistent pitching rates and improved fermentation performance.
