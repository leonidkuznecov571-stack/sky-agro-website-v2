Serving Tanks

Capacity: 200–2,000 L

Serving tanks are vessels used for final storage and direct dispensing of finished beverages, typically installed in taprooms, bars, or hospitality environments. They allow beer or other drinks to be served directly from the tank, reducing handling and preserving product freshness.

Manufactured in stainless steel and available in insulated or single-wall formats, serving tanks provide a practical solution for maintaining product quality while simplifying dispense systems.

Dispense Efficiency & Freshness

  1. 01

    Direct-to-Tap Serving

    Eliminates the need for kegs by allowing product to be dispensed directly from the tank, reducing handling and labour.

  2. 02

    Reduced Oxygen Exposure

    Closed-system serving minimises oxidation, helping maintain flavour stability and product quality.

  3. 03

    Consistent Serving Conditions

    Temperature and pressure control ensure stable carbonation and consistent pour quality.

Operational Simplicity

  1. 04

    Reduced Packaging Requirements

    Removes reliance on kegs or intermediate packaging, lowering operational complexity and cost.

  2. 05

    Compact Installation Options

    Suitable for installation in visible or back-of-house environments, including stacked or horizontal layouts where space is limited.

  3. 06

    Easy Cleaning & Maintenance

    CIP-compatible design simplifies cleaning cycles, supporting hygiene without disrupting service operations.

Four available configurations

  1. Single-Wall Serving Tank

    Non-insulated tank for environments with external temperature control, typically used in bars or taprooms for short-term serving.

  2. Insulated Serving Tank

    Fully insulated configuration maintaining product temperature independently, suitable for venues without controlled ambient conditions.

  3. Horizontal Serving Tank

    Designed for low-height installations, often used in under-bar or cellar applications with limited vertical space.

  4. Stacked Serving Tank System

    Multiple tanks configured vertically to maximise serving capacity within constrained floor areas.
