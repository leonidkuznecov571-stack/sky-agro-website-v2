01 - Design

Built for service and presentation

  1. 01

    Insulated for passive temperature stability

    Polyurethane foam insulation maintains serving temperature without continuous active cooling, reducing energy draw and keeping beer in condition during quiet service periods.

  2. 02

    Low dead-leg fittings

    Sanitary outlet fittings are positioned to minimise product hold-up, reducing the risk of stale pockets and simplifying CIP cleaning between products or brands.

  3. 03

    Glycol jacket option

    Active glycol jacket for environments where ambient temperature fluctuates or rapid chilling between fills is required. Fully compatible with standard brewery glycol systems.
