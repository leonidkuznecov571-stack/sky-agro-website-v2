01 - Design

Built for food-grade process demands

  1. 01

    Food-grade internal finish

    Electropolished or mechanically polished interior surfaces meet the cleanliness demands of dairy and liquid-food production, reducing biofilm risk and supporting effective CIP cleaning.

  2. 02

    Agitator-ready construction

    Top-mounted agitator stub and reinforced dished lid allow fitment of direct-drive or gearbox agitators without structural modification, suited to mixing, homogenisation or gentle blending duties.

  3. 03

    Consistent temperature stability

    PU foam insulation in standard configurations maintains product temperature during storage, reducing energy use. Jacketed variants support active heating or cooling for process-critical applications.
