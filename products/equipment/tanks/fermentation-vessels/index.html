Cellar vessels

Fermentation Vessels

Capacity: 500–20,000 L

Conical fermentation tanks designed for controlled primary fermentation, reliable yeast management and consistent temperature stability across your cellar. Built in food-grade stainless steel to customer volume specifications, with jacket and insulation options to suit any brewery environment.

Designed for practical cellar operation, vessels include sanitary CIP, integrated sampling and racking, and pressure-rated fittings for controlled fermentation. The 60° cone supports clean yeast harvesting and full drainage, while glycol jackets and insulation maintain stable conditions through fermentation and cold crashing. Configurable for indoor or outdoor installation, with scalable capacity and fitting options.

Built for consistent fermentation

  1. 01

    Precise temperature control

    Glycol-fed jacket segments covering the cone and body allow independent zone control, keeping fermentation temperature stable from pitch to crash cool without relying on ambient cellar conditions.

  2. 02

    60° conical base for clean yeast cropping

    The steep conical base promotes reliable yeast sedimentation, making successive crops straightforward and reducing carry-over into the finished beer.

  3. 03

    CIP-ready fittings as standard

    Spray-ball inlet, drain, and sample valve positions follow standard hygienic fitting layouts so the vessel integrates directly with CIP circuits without additional modification.

Cellar efficiency and control

  1. 04

    Pressure-rated for flexible operation

    Designed as a pressure vessel, allowing spunding, closed transfers, and in-vessel carbonation. This reduces oxygen pickup, supports CO₂ retention, and improves overall product stability from fermentation through to transfer.

  2. 05

    Full-drain, hygienic construction

    60° conical base and flush-ground internal welds eliminate dead zones, ensuring complete drainage and consistent clean-in-place performance. Hygienic design reduces contamination risk and supports repeatable batch quality.

  3. 06

    Integrated sampling and control

    Sanitary sample valve and side-mounted thermowell enable accurate in-process monitoring without opening the vessel. Supports consistent fermentation management and reliable quality control across batches.

Four configurations available

  1. CCV – Indoor

    Standard cylindroconical fermenter for controlled cellar environments. Fully jacketed and insulated, suited to primary fermentation, conditioning, and cold crashing in indoor installations.

  2. CCV – Outdoor

    Same specification as indoor vessels, designed for external installation without additional casing. Suitable where indoor space is limited or capacity is expanded beyond the building footprint.

  3. Open-top fermenter

    Traditional open vessel for top-fermenting styles. Supports yeast cropping from the surface and is suited to breweries producing cask ales or styles requiring open fermentation character.

  4. Yorkshire Square

    Two-chamber fermentation system for classic Northern English ales. Upper chamber promotes yeast head formation and top-cropping, enabling distinct fermentation profiles with traditional ale strains.
