HTST treatment with process control

  1. 01

    Precise PU control

    Free PU setting, automatic control, variable flow compatibility, and temperature adjustment allow the pasteurisation process to be matched to beer, RTD, seltzer, and soft-drink requirements.

  2. 02

    Efficient heat recovery

    The plate heat exchanger and loop design recover heat between cold incoming product and hot pasteurised product, reducing utility demand and improving operating efficiency.

  3. 03

    Hygienic, compact skid design

    Hard piping, reduced dead legs, self-draining capability, CIP compatibility, and a plug-and-play skid format support sanitary operation with a small footprint.
