Auto CIP Station

Full automatic control: PLC/HMI operation with remote monitoring options for repeatable CIP programmes.

A modular automated CIP station for breweries, beverage plants, and food-processing sites requiring repeatable cleaning of tanks, pipework, filling machines, and production units. Supplied as a compact plug-and-play skid, the system integrates tanks, pumps, instrumentation, heat exchange, automatic valves, and PLC/HMI control.

Automated sanitation for process reliability

  1. 01

    Repeatable cleaning parameters

    Cleaning time, temperature, pH, pressure, and flow can be adjusted to match the equipment, soil load, and cleaning programme, helping operators standardise sanitation across production.

  2. 02

    Compact plug-and-play installation

    The skid is pre-assembled with pipelines, plate heat exchangers, pumps, and instrumentation, reducing site installation complexity and supporting faster commissioning.

  3. 03

    Lower manual intervention

    Automatic valves, instrumentation, and PLC/HMI control reduce operator handling during cleaning cycles, improving process consistency and helping minimise day-to-day maintenance requirements.
