Beverage Production Line

Multi-stream inline blending: deaerated water plus up to four additional ingredients can be dosed continuously into a static mixing unit.

A flexible inline beverage production line for RTDs, alcoholic drinks, non-alcoholic beverages, soft drinks, and carbonated products. The system combines multi-stream dosing, recipe-based blending, temperature control, crash cooling, inline carbonation, flash pasteurisation, buffer tanks, dispensing, and automated CIP routing.

Integrated beverage preparation from dosing to dispensing

  1. 01

    Flexible product platform

    Multi-stream dosing allows the same line to support a wide range of beverage types, including RTDs, soft drinks, alcoholic, and non-alcoholic products.

  2. 02

    Recipe-based consistency

    HMI-selected recipes control proportional blending, gas dosing, tank switching, and process settings, helping maintain repeatable product quality across batches.

  3. 03

    Complete inline process integration

    Blending, cooling, carbonation, pasteurisation, storage, dispensing, and CIP routing are integrated into one production concept, reducing transfers and limiting product loss.
