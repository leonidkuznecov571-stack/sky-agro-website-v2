Aquatreat

Aquatreat is a sulphuric acid-based solution for brewing liquor treatment, used to reduce alkalinity while increasing sulphate levels. It supports mash performance and enhances hop bitterness and dryness, making it a practical choice for adjusting water profile in hop-forward styles.

Aqua Treat is a food-grade acid-based solution developed for water treatment applications in the brewing industry. Aqua Treat Water Treatment is designed to reduce alkalinity and optimise brewing liquor composition, ensuring consistent process performance and product quality.

Based on a sulphuric acid formulation, Aqua Treat effectively lowers pH and introduces sulphate ions into the brewing liquor. This supports improved mash performance, enhances wort clarity, and contributes to better filtration, extract yield, and fermentability.

The product is particularly effective in helping maintain optimal process conditions throughout brewing and can be used for both initial water treatment and fine pH adjustment. It is recommended to dose into cold liquor tanks to prevent scale build-up and allow proper reaction with water carbonates.

This brewing water treatment solution is suitable for use in breweries and beverage production facilities where precise control of water chemistry is essential.

Aqua Treat (Water Treatment) is designed for controlled alkalinity reduction and water chemistry optimisation, and is suitable for:

  • Brewing water treatment
  • Liquor tank dosing (cold liquor tanks)
  • pH adjustment processes
  • Process water conditioning
  • Breweries and beverage production plants

Aqua Treat is an acidic product and must be handled with care.

  • Causes skin irritation (H315)
  • Causes serious eye irritation (H319)

For full information, please refer to SDS.

Safety precautions:

  • Wear protective gloves, protective clothing, and eye/face protection
  • Avoid contact with skin and eyes
  • Do not breathe vapours or spray
  • Use only in well-ventilated areas
  • In case of exposure, rinse thoroughly with water and seek medical attention if irritation persists.

Handling and Storage:

  • Store in a dry, cool, well-ventilated place
  • Keep container tightly closed
  • Store in original container
  • Keep away from incompatible materials (e.g. chlorines and strong bases)
  • Avoid direct sunlight and freezing
  • Use appropriate protective equipment during handling
