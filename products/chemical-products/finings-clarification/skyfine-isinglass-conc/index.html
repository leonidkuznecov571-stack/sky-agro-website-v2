SkyFine Isinglass Conc is a concentrated version of traditional isinglass finings, designed for dilution prior to use. Like RTU variants, it binds yeast cells through electrostatic interaction, promoting rapid clarification in both cask and tank-conditioned beer.

Its concentrated format makes it a cost-effective option for higher-volume breweries, allowing flexible preparation and dosing. When correctly diluted and applied, it delivers strong clarification performance with minimal product loss.
