Sky Tabs

Sky Tabs are carrageenan-based kettle finings for improved wort clarification and protein removal.

Packaging
£48.00 / 2 kg
Request pricing
Request SDS Download TDS

Sky Tabs are made from refined carrageenan and are added during the final stages of the boil to bind haze-forming proteins. This promotes a well-defined hot break and reduces protein carryover into fermentation.

Improved wort clarity supports better yeast performance, reduced conditioning time and enhanced filterability. The tablet format allows convenient, precise dosing, particularly for smaller or manual brewing operations.

Packaging options Available in 2 kg, 5 kg and 20 kg packs.
Status Technical data and dosing guidance to be confirmed with the SKY LABS team.
