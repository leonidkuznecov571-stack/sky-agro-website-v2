Caustic Plus is a high-performance sodium hydroxide-based alkaline cleaner designed for cleaning applications in the food and beverage industry. The product is specifically developed for use in CIP (Clean-in-Place) systems, ensuring effective removal of organic residues, fats, and protein-based soils from processing equipment.

This alkaline CIP (Caustic Plus Alkaline Cleaner) detergent is suitable for use in breweries, dairy processing facilities, and general food production plants where high hygiene standards are essential. It is effective at both ambient and elevated temperatures, providing flexible and efficient cleaning performance for industrial operations.

Formulated with sodium hydroxide and sequestering agents, Caustic Plus delivers reliable cleaning performance even in demanding industrial environments, including areas with hard water. The product is particularly effective in brewery cleaning applications and can be used across a wide range of process equipment.

Caustic Plus is suitable for use in:

  • CIP cleaning systems
  • Brewing equipment, vessels, and tanks
  • Dairy processing installations
  • Food production lines
  • Pipework and process systems
  • Removal of fats, proteins, and organic residues

Caustic Plus is a hazardous alkaline product and must be handled with care.

  • Causes severe skin burns and eye damage (H314)
  • Corrosive product

For full information, please refer to SDS.

Safety precautions:

  • Do not breathe vapour or spray
  • Wear protective gloves, protective clothing, and eye/face protection
  • Use only in well-ventilated areas
  • Avoid contact with skin and eyes In case of exposure, follow first aid instructions and seek medical attention immediately.

Handling and Storage:

  • Store in a cool, well-ventilated place
  • Keep container tightly closed
  • Store in original container
  • Keep away from acids
  • Avoid direct sunlight and freezing
  • Use appropriate protective equipment during handling
