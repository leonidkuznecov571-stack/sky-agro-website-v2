Enzybrew L is a professional-grade, liquid caustic cleaning formulation designed specifically for the brewing and beverage sector. Based on sodium hydroxide enhanced with gluconate chelating agents and non-ionic surfactants, it delivers exceptional cleaning performance against proteinaceous soils, hop resins, and yeast cell debris — the most demanding fouling types encountered in brewery CIP circuits.

Unlike standard caustic solutions, Enzybrew L remains effective in moderate hardness water (up to 250 ppm as CaCO₃) without the need for water softening pre-treatment, which reduces operating costs in regions with harder water supplies. The wetting agent package ensures rapid, even soil penetration at lower temperatures, enabling effective cleaning at 60 °C rather than the 80 °C typically required by unformulated caustic.

Compatible with stainless steel, food-grade plastics (HDPE, PP, PVDF), and rubber gasket materials. Not recommended for use on aluminium, zinc-coated, or galvanised surfaces.

Enzybrew L is the liquid format enzymatic cleaner, offering the same enzymatic soil removal performance as Enzybrew 10 with easier dosing into automated CIP systems. It is particularly suited to high-throughput breweries with continuous cleaning requirements.

  • Fermentation vessel cleaning between batches
  • CIP of yeast-handling equipment (centrifuges, propagators, collection vessels)
  • Cask and container cleaning where enzymatic action gives an advantage over standard caustic
  • Automated CIP systems requiring precise liquid dosing of enzymatic product
  • Low-temperature cleaning regimes where heated CIP is not available

Recommended working concentration: 0.5–2% v/v at 35–55 °C for optimum enzymatic activity. Consult the TDS for specific dosage guidance by application type.
