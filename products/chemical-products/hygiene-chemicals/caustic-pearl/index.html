Caustic Pearl

Caustic Pearl is a solid sodium hydroxide product with high purity, widely used as a base alkaline cleaner in brewery CIP. It efficiently breaks down proteins, starch and fats across vessels, pipework and brewhouse equipment, forming the foundation of many cleaning programmes.

Its solid format provides flexibility in storage, transport and dosing, making it suitable for breweries managing their own cleaning formulations. When combined with sequestrants in hard water areas, it ensures consistent performance while allowing full control over chemical strength and cost optimisation.

Caustic Pearl is suited to breweries that formulate and manage their own CIP cleaning solutions on site. Its solid format gives precise control over concentration and cost, and simplifies storage and transport compared to bulk liquids.

  • In-house preparation of alkaline CIP cleaning solutions
  • Fermentation vessel, conditioning tank, and bright beer tank cleaning
  • Brewhouse vessel cleaning (mash tuns, lauter tuns, kettles, whirlpools)
  • Process pipework and fittings
  • Sites where flexibility in formulation strength and cost management is a priority

Dissolve to the required working concentration before use. Combine with an appropriate sequestrant in hard water areas. Consult the TDS for dosage guidance.
