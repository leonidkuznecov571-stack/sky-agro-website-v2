Water Test Strips

Routine water-quality screening

Water test strips give brewery teams a quick check on incoming water, rinse water, and utility streams where a fast visual result is useful. They are suited to routine monitoring and troubleshooting when operators want a rapid indication before moving to full analytical testing.

Water test strips provide a practical first-line check when breweries want to monitor routine water quality without sending every sample for laboratory analysis. They are useful for quick trend checks on hardness, alkalinity, or similar water parameters depending on the strip grade supplied.

They support day-to-day decision-making around liquor preparation, rinse checks, and utility monitoring, especially where a pass/fail or indicative result is sufficient.

Exact parameter ranges vary by strip type and should be confirmed against the supplied pack insert.

Expose the strip to the sample as directed, wait the recommended read time, and compare the pad colours with the supplied scale. Use clean sample containers and avoid cross-contaminating the strip pack during routine testing.

Where a measured result is critical to recipe or compliance decisions, confirm the reading with the appropriate calibrated instrument or laboratory test.

Storage Store dry, sealed, and away from direct sunlight, steam, and damp environments.
