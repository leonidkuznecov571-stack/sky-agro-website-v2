Glycol (MPG) is a mono propylene glycol heat-transfer fluid intended for dilution and use in closed-loop brewery cooling systems. It supports stable cooling performance in fermenter jackets, cellar circuits, and packaged refrigeration skids.

The product is suitable for sites that want a food-grade glycol option for process-adjacent cooling infrastructure, with dilution selected according to the lowest operating temperature in the system.

Target dilution and freeze protection should be selected to match the site’s operating conditions.

Charge into a clean closed loop at the dilution appropriate to the target operating temperature. Check inhibitor requirements, seal compatibility, and overall system condition before filling or topping up.

For mixed-metal systems or sites with water-quality concerns, contact the team for guidance on dilution water and maintenance checks.

Storage Store sealed, clean, and protected from contamination and extreme temperatures.
