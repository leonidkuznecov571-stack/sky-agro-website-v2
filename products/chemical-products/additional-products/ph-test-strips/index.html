pH Test Strips provide a simple visual check of acidity or alkalinity across a wide range of operational tasks in breweries and beverage plants. They are useful for rinse-water checks, liquor preparation, sample screening, and quick bench comparisons.

They are intended for rapid operational checks rather than replacing a calibrated pH meter where higher precision is required.
