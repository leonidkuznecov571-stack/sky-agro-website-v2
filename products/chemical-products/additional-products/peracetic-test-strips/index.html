Peracetic Test Strips are designed for quick spot-checking of peracetic acid working solutions used in brewery cleaning and sanitising routines. They help operators verify that day-to-day plant hygiene chemistry is being made up and maintained at the intended strength.

They are useful for packaging lines, rinsers, cellar tasks, and general hygiene monitoring where a fast visual pass/fail style check is preferred over slower laboratory methods.

Measurement range and colour scale should be confirmed against the supplied batch instructions.

Dip or expose the strip to the sample as directed, wait the specified read time, and compare the colour change with the supplied chart. Use fresh strips only and keep containers closed between checks.

These strips are intended for operational verification, not as a substitute for a full analytical quality-control method where formal records are required.
