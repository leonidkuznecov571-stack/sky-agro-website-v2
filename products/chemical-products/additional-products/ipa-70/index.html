IPA 70 (Ready-to-Use Isopropyl Alcohol-Based Surface Disinfectant) is a ready-to-use alcohol-based disinfectant developed for rapid surface sanitation in the food and beverage industry. The product provides effective microbial control and is designed for direct application without dilution.

A 70% isopropyl alcohol solution is recognised as the most effective concentration for disinfection, as the presence of water enhances protein denaturation and improves penetration into microbial cells. This ensures efficient destruction of bacteria and other microorganisms while increasing contact time on surfaces.

IPA 70 combines strong disinfecting performance with rapid evaporation, leaving no residue and eliminating the need for rinsing. It can be applied using spray systems or cloths, making it ideal for quick and efficient sanitation in operational environments.

IPA 70 (Isopropyl Alcohol Disinfectant) is designed for rapid disinfection of surfaces where immediate use and fast drying are required, and is suitable for:

  • Surface disinfection of equipment and tools
  • Food processing environments
  • Brewing and beverage production facilities
  • Manual cleaning and sanitation
  • Quick hygiene control applications

IPA (Isopropyl Alcohol Disinfectant) 70 is a highly flammable liquid and must be handled with care.

  • Highly flammable liquid and vapour (H225)
  • Causes serious eye irritation (H319)
  • May cause drowsiness or dizziness (H336)

Safety precautions:

  • Keep away from heat, sparks, open flames, and hot surfaces
  • Do not smoke during use
  • Use only in well-ventilated areas
  • Avoid breathing vapour or spray
  • Wear protective gloves and eye protection
  • In case of exposure, follow first aid instructions and seek medical attention if symptoms persist.

Handling and Storage:

  • Store in a cool, well-ventilated place
  • Keep container tightly closed
  • Store in original container
  • Keep away from heat, sparks, and ignition sources
  • Keep away from oxidising agents
  • Avoid direct sunlight and freezing
  • Use appropriate protective equipment during handling
