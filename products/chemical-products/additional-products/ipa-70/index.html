IPA 70% is a ready-to-use isopropyl alcohol solution formulated for rapid surface sanitisation and general technical cleaning in food and beverage production environments. The 70% v/v concentration is the internationally recognised optimum for antimicrobial efficacy — higher concentrations denature surface proteins before they can penetrate the cell wall, reducing kill rate.

The product is fast-acting with a typical contact time of under 30 seconds on hard, non-porous surfaces. It leaves no detectable residue after evaporation and does not require a water rinse, making it highly practical for use on small-bore fittings, sampling valves, instrumentation, and any surface where water carry-over is undesirable.

Not intended to replace periodic CIP cleaning programmes. For use as a supplementary, between-batch sanitiser on surfaces that have already been cleaned. Not suitable for use on copper or certain polymers — check compatibility before first use.

Apply IPA 70% to a clean surface using a pump-action spray bottle, trigger sprayer, or lint-free wipe. Ensure the surface is free from gross soiling and residual cleaning chemicals before application. Allow the product to remain wet on the surface for a minimum of 30 seconds before allowing to evaporate. Do not rinse.

Typical applications include sampling valves, sight glasses, hose ends, temperature probes, pipework unions, filling heads, and laboratory bench surfaces. Use in a well-ventilated area and away from open flames or ignition sources.

Storage Store in a cool, well-ventilated area away from heat, sparks, and open flames. Keep containers tightly closed. Store separately from oxidising agents and strong acids.
Shelf life 24 months from date of manufacture in original, unopened containers.
Regulatory Classified as flammable liquid, Category 2 (GHS02). Refer to SDS for full ADR/IMDG transport classification before shipping.
PPE required Chemical-resistant gloves, eye protection. Ensure adequate ventilation. Avoid prolonged skin contact and inhalation of vapour.
