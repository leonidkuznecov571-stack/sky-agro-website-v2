Phenolphthalein test strips are used for rinse verification after alkaline cleaning. They help brewery teams confirm that residual caustic has been removed from tanks, lines, fittings, and packaging components before product contact resumes.

They are especially useful in places where a quick visual check is needed during shutdown, turnaround, or routine sanitation tasks.
