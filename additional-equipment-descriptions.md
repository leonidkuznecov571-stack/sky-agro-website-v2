# Processing, Cleaning, Packaging and Beverage Systems

## CIP Cart

The CIP Cart is a compact, portable clean-in-place unit for cleaning vessels, pipework, heat exchangers, and ancillary process equipment without manual disassembly. It is designed for breweries and beverage producers that need a flexible cleaning solution for single vessels, smaller cellars, or equipment located away from a fixed CIP manifold.

Built in food-grade stainless steel, the system uses dedicated cleaning tanks, temperature control, a variable-speed pump, and directional valves for recirculation and transfer to target equipment. Standard caustic and sanitiser tank sizes include 50 L, 100 L, and 200 L, with custom sizes available where cleaning volume or site layout requires it.

### Advantages

#### Mobility & Compatibility

**Portable Cleaning**  
The mobile frame allows the unit to be moved between fermenters, BBTs, pipework, and other process equipment, avoiding the need for a fixed CIP station at every location.

**Broad Equipment Compatibility**  
Suitable for breweries, beverage plants, dairy installations, and food processing environments, making it a practical utility unit across mixed production areas.

**Useful for Smaller Sites**  
Well suited to breweries with limited floor space, lower tank counts, or equipment positioned outside the main cellar cleaning circuit.

#### Cleaning Performance & Safety

**Repeatable Cleaning Cycles**  
Controlled circulation, heating, and chemical contact time support more consistent cleaning than manual hose-based washing, improving hygiene between production runs.

**Temperature Control**  
Heated tanks and digital temperature control help maintain caustic solution temperature during the cleaning process, improving cleaning effectiveness on organic residues.

**Dry-Fire Protection**  
Inline liquid sensors and temperature probes protect heating elements from dry-firing, improving operational safety and reducing avoidable equipment damage.

### Configurations

**50 L CIP Cart**  
Compact portable configuration for smaller vessels, pilot systems, and occasional cleaning duties where chemical volume and available cellar space are limited.

**100 L CIP Cart**  
General-purpose brewery configuration for routine cleaning of fermenters, BBTs, short pipe runs, and ancillary equipment in smaller production environments.

**200 L CIP Cart**  
Higher-volume cart for larger vessels or longer cleaning loops, providing greater solution capacity while remaining mobile and flexible.

**Custom CIP Cart**  
Built to suit specific tank counts, vessel sizes, cleaning circuits, and chemical programmes where standard tank volumes are not sufficient.

## Auto CIP Station

The Auto CIP Station is a fixed, automated clean-in-place system for targeted cleaning of tanks, pipework, filling machines, and other production equipment. It is designed for breweries and beverage plants that require programmable, repeatable cleaning across multiple circuits with reduced operator intervention.

Cleaning parameters including time, temperature, flow rate, pressure, pH, and conductivity can be configured around the process requirement. The system is supplied as a pre-assembled unit with stainless steel tanks, pumps, valve manifolds, dosing equipment, heat exchanger, instrumentation, PLC, and HMI control.

### Advantages

#### Automation & Control

**Programmable Cleaning**  
Cleaning loops and process steps can be selected through the HMI, allowing repeatable programmes for different tanks, pipework sections, or production equipment.

**Parameter Monitoring**  
Flow, temperature, pressure, pH, conductivity, and tank level instrumentation give operators better visibility over cleaning performance and chemical condition.

**Reduced Manual Error**  
Automated valve routing and recipe-based operation reduce reliance on manual valve changes, helping prevent missed steps or incorrect chemical routing.

#### Hygiene & Resource Management

**Multi-Circuit Cleaning**  
Designed for fixed installations with several cleaning circuits, allowing efficient management of larger production areas from one central CIP station.

**Chemical Recovery**  
Recovery tanks and return circuits allow suitable cleaning solutions to be recaptured and reused, reducing chemical loss and operating cost.

**Integrated Dosing and Heating**  
Chemical dosing and plate heat exchanger heating support controlled caustic, acid, and sanitiser preparation within the system.

### Configurations

**Three-Tank CIP Station**  
Typical compact automated configuration using rinse water, caustic, and sanitiser or acid tanks for essential brewery and beverage cleaning routines.

**Four-Tank CIP Station**  
Adds an additional tank for acid, recovery, or dedicated sterilant, supporting more complete cleaning programmes and better chemical segregation.

**Five-Tank CIP Station**  
Full automated configuration with water, caustic, acid, steriliser, and recovery tanks for larger multi-loop production installations.

**Custom Automated CIP System**  
Built around the site’s process layout, number of tanks, filling equipment, cleaning loops, required dosing points, and automation level.

## Cabinet Pasteuriser

The Cabinet Pasteuriser is a batch pasteurisation unit for bottled and canned beer, milk, and other beverages. Manufactured in SS304 stainless steel, it uses steam within an enclosed chamber to heat packaged product under controlled conditions, with pasteurisation temperature and holding time set according to the product requirement.

It is suited to smaller production runs, speciality products, and operations where batch treatment is preferable to inline pasteurisation. The system supports adjustable temperature and time control, uniform steam exposure inside the cabinet, and controlled cooling after the holding phase.

### Advantages

#### Batch Pasteurisation

**Packaged Product Treatment**  
Pasteurises bottled or canned product after filling, making it suitable for beer, milk, and other beverages requiring shelf-life improvement in final container format.

**Adjustable Time and Temperature**  
Operators can set pasteurisation parameters according to product type, packaging format, and required pasteurisation units.

**Uniform Heat Exposure**  
Steam circulation inside the cabinet supports even heat distribution across the batch, reducing variation between containers.

#### Operation & Safety

**Compact Batch Format**  
Suitable for smaller breweries or beverage producers that do not require continuous inline pasteurisation capacity.

**Automatic Temperature Control**  
Temperature is automatically controlled during the cycle, reducing operator workload and improving repeatability.

**Controlled Cooling Phase**  
Cooling water brings product back towards ambient temperature after pasteurisation, reducing handling risk and preparing product for storage.

### Configurations

**Single-Cabin Pasteuriser**  
Compact cabinet configuration for smaller batch volumes, typically used by craft breweries or beverage producers with moderate packaged product throughput.

**Double-Cabin Pasteuriser**  
Higher-capacity cabinet configuration with two batch chambers, increasing packaged product volume per cycle while retaining batch pasteurisation control.

**Electric Heating Configuration**  
Uses integrated electric heating where steam supply is unavailable or where a self-contained pasteurisation unit is preferred.

**Steam-Supply Configuration**  
Uses customer-supplied or integrated steam generation depending on site utilities, production volume, and installation requirements.

## Flash Pasteuriser

The Flash Pasteuriser is an inline HTST pasteurisation system for beer, RTDs, soft drinks, seltzers, and other carbonated or non-carbonated beverages. Product is heated, held at pasteurisation temperature, and rapidly cooled in a continuous process before packaging, avoiding the longer cycle times associated with batch pasteurisation.

The system uses plate heat exchanger zones for heat recovery, final heating, holding, and cooling. PLC/HMI control manages pasteurisation settings, temperature control, alarms, and diversion logic, while hygienic hard piping and CIP compatibility support integration into commercial beverage lines.

### Advantages

#### Process Efficiency

**Continuous Pasteurisation**  
Inline operation treats product continuously before packaging, supporting higher throughput and reducing the waiting time associated with batch cabinet systems.

**Heat Recovery**  
Plate heat exchanger design recovers heat from outgoing pasteurised product to pre-heat incoming product, reducing heating and cooling demand.

**Rapid Heating and Cooling**  
HTST treatment limits thermal exposure while still achieving the required pasteurisation effect, helping protect product flavour and carbonation stability.

#### Control & Hygiene

**Precise PU Control**  
PLC/HMI control allows pasteurisation temperature, holding time, and process settings to be managed consistently across production runs.

**Automatic Diversion Protection**  
If process temperature or pressure deviates from set limits, product can be diverted to recirculation rather than passing forward to packaging.

**CIP-Compatible Design**  
Hygienic pipework, plate heat exchanger construction, and clean-in-place compatibility support routine sanitation without dismantling process sections.

### Configurations

**Standard Flash Pasteuriser**  
Inline HTST skid for beer and beverage production, integrating heating, holding, cooling, instrumentation, and automated process control.

**High-Recovery Flash Pasteuriser**  
Configuration focused on energy efficiency, using plate heat exchanger recovery loops to minimise steam and glycol demand.

**Carbonated Beverage Configuration**  
Designed for beer, seltzer, RTD, and soda applications where pressure control and gentle handling are required to protect carbonation.

**Custom Capacity Flash Pasteuriser**  
Specified to suit target throughput, product type, pasteurisation units, utility availability, and integration with the filling line.

## Manual Blending and Carbonation System

The Manual Blending and Carbonation System is a compact, mobile inline unit for CO₂ infusion and liquid additive blending in beer and carbonated beverages. It is designed for small-batch production, product trials, and production environments where manual flow control is acceptable.

CO₂ is introduced through a Venturi injector and mixed with the product stream using a sanitary static packing element. Operators control product flow, CO₂ flow, liquid pressure, and additive dosing manually using flow metres, making the system suitable for beer, sparkling water, soda, and RTD beverage applications.

### Advantages

#### Flexible Production

**Mobile Format**  
Wheel-mounted construction allows the unit to be moved between production points, avoiding fixed installation and supporting flexible small-batch work.

**Manual Process Control**  
Flow metres give operators direct control over product, CO₂, and additive streams, useful for trials and low-volume production.

**Broad Beverage Compatibility**  
Suitable for beer, sparkling water, soda, and other carbonated beverages requiring inline gas infusion or liquid additive blending.

#### Mixing & Hygiene

**Venturi CO₂ Injection**  
Venturi injection disperses CO₂ into the product stream quickly, supporting controlled carbonation without requiring large batch conditioning times.

**Static Mixing Element**  
Sanitary corrugated packing improves contact between liquid streams and gas, helping achieve more uniform blending and carbonation.

**CIP-Compatible Construction**  
The packing element and wetted surfaces are clean-in-place compatible, reducing dismantling and limiting hygiene risks around the mixing section.

### Configurations

**Manual Carbonation Unit**  
Compact inline unit focused on CO₂ infusion for beer and carbonated beverages, with manual adjustment of gas and liquid flow.

**Manual Blending Unit**  
Configuration for liquid additive blending where flavour, syrup, or other streams are dosed manually into a base liquid.

**Mobile Trial System**  
Wheel-mounted arrangement for pilot batches, product development, and temporary production locations where fixed pipework is not required.

## Automated Blending and Carbonation System

The Automated Blending and Carbonation System combines inline liquid blending with controlled CO₂ or N₂ dosing for beer, soft drinks, RTDs, seltzers, and other carbonated beverages. It is designed to reduce batch carbonation downtime and improve repeatability through continuous inline operation.

Semi-automatic and fully automatic versions share the same core principle: product flows continuously through the system while gas is dispersed via PIN-POINT micro-pore stainless steel injector plates. Depending on automation level, liquid ratios, gas dosing, pressure control, and recipe settings are managed through instrumentation, PID valves, and PLC/HMI control.

### Advantages

#### Carbonation Accuracy

**Microbubble CO₂ Injection**  
PIN-POINT injector plates disperse CO₂ into fine bubbles, increasing gas-liquid contact area and supporting efficient mass transfer into the product.

**Inline Continuous Operation**  
Continuous carbonation removes the waiting time of batch carbonation, allowing product to move through blending, carbonation, and packaging more efficiently.

**Recipe-Based Control**  
Automatic configurations allow carbonation level and blending ratios to be stored and repeated through PLC/HMI recipes.

#### Blending & Production Control

**Automated Ratio Management**  
Liquid streams can be controlled with feed pumps, flow metres, and PID valves, improving consistency across product recipes.

**Compact Skid Design**  
Small footprint makes the system suitable for integration into existing production areas without requiring a full beverage plant rebuild.

**CIP-Compatible Pipeline**  
Hygienic stainless steel pipework and clean-in-place compatibility support routine cleaning between products or batches.

### Configurations

**Semi-Auto Carbonation System**  
Automated liquid and flavour ratio control with manual CO₂ and pressure adjustment, suited to breweries wanting improved consistency without full automation.

**Fully Automatic Carbonation System**  
PLC/HMI-controlled inline carbonation with automatic gas dosing, recipe storage, and integrated flow regulation for repeatable production runs.

**Two-Stream Blending System**  
Standard configuration for base liquid plus flavour, syrup, alcohol, or other liquid additive, with automatic flow and ratio control.

**Custom Multi-Stream System**  
Expanded configuration for additional liquid streams, nitrogen dosing, or powder dosing where product range or recipe complexity requires it.

## Beverage Production Line

The Beverage Production Line is an integrated inline processing system for high-flexibility beverage production. It combines proportional blending, temperature control, carbonation, optional pasteurisation, and dispensing or buffer tank management within a continuous process.

Deaerated water and up to four additional ingredient streams, including syrup, flavour, wine, spirits, or other liquids, are dosed into a static mixing unit according to HMI-selected recipes. The line is suited to RTDs, soft drinks, alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages, and carbonated products requiring repeatable blending with minimal product loss.

### Advantages

#### Multi-Stage Production

**Integrated Processing**  
Blending, carbonation, temperature control, pasteurisation, and tank management can be combined into one continuous line, reducing transfer steps and product handling.

**Multi-Stream Blending**  
Up to four ingredient streams plus deaerated water can be metered into the system, supporting a wide range of recipes and beverage formats.

**Batch or Continuous Operation**  
The line can support both batch and continuous production modes, giving producers flexibility across product types and production volumes.

#### Control & Product Consistency

**Recipe-Based HMI Operation**  
Operators select recipes through the HMI, with ingredient dosing, carbonation, and tank routing controlled according to programmed parameters.

**Automated Tank Switching**  
Buffer and storage tanks can be managed so that one tank supplies the filler while another receives blended product, reducing process interruptions.

**Low Product Loss**  
Inline dosing and controlled tank management reduce unnecessary transfers, helping preserve product volume and quality during changeovers.

### Configurations

**Blending and Carbonation Line**  
Core line combining multi-stream proportional blending with inline CO₂ or N₂ dosing for carbonated beverage production.

**Blending, Carbonation and Pasteurisation Line**  
Integrated configuration adding flash pasteurisation for products requiring microbial control prior to filling.

**RTD Beverage Production Line**  
Recipe-led system for alcoholic and non-alcoholic ready-to-drink products using multiple liquid streams and controlled carbonation.

**Custom Beverage Plant**  
Full production-line configuration specified around ingredient streams, pasteurisation needs, tank volumes, filling equipment, automation level, and site layout.

## Dealcoholiser

The Dealcoholiser is a skid-mounted spiral reverse osmosis membrane system for reducing the alcohol content of fully fermented beer and other low-alcohol fermented products. It operates without heat input, using pressure-driven separation to remove ethanol and water while retaining heavier flavour-active compounds in the retentate.

The system is suited to breweries producing low- and no-alcohol beer where aroma, body, and base beer character need to be preserved. Capacity is scalable by adding membranes in parallel, and the process can run in batch or continuous mode with dedicated CIP loops for membranes and process pipework.

### Advantages

#### Alcohol Reduction & Product Quality

**Low-Temperature Processing**  
Reverse osmosis removes alcohol without heat input, helping preserve delicate flavour compounds that can be damaged by thermal dealcoholisation.

**ABV Control**  
Final ABV is managed through membrane selection, operating pressure, diafiltration volume, and dilution strategy, allowing production of low- and no-alcohol formats.

**Flavour Retention**  
The membrane process retains many heavier beer compounds in the retentate, helping the finished product remain closer to the original beer profile.

#### Scalability & Operation

**Scalable Membrane Capacity**  
Throughput can be increased by adding membrane modules in parallel, allowing capacity to grow with market demand.

**Batch or Continuous Modes**  
Dual operating modes support both smaller production runs and more continuous production planning.

**Dedicated CIP Loops**  
Separate cleaning circuits for RO membranes and process pipework help maintain hygiene while protecting membrane performance.

### Configurations

**Single-Membrane Dealcoholiser**  
Base skid configuration for smaller production volumes, typically suitable for pilot, craft, or lower-throughput no-alcohol beer programmes.

**Multi-Membrane Dealcoholiser**  
Expanded configuration with membrane modules in parallel, increasing throughput while retaining the same reverse osmosis process principle.

**Semi-Automatic Dealcoholiser**  
Operator-supervised configuration with PLC-assisted control over key process variables, suitable where manual oversight is acceptable.

**Fully Automatic Dealcoholiser**  
Higher-automation configuration with process supervision, flow regulation, pressure monitoring, and automated cleaning routines for more repeatable operation.

## Keg Washer

The Keg Washer is a two-station, fully automatic keg cleaning unit for breweries using Euro or US standard kegs from 10 L to 60 L. Kegs are loaded manually, after which the connector locks the spear and the cleaning programme runs automatically.

The machine uses two independent cleaning stations, SS304 cleaning tanks, centrifugal pumps, high- and low-pressure pulsed valves, and Siemens PLC control. Heating is available by steam or electric element, and spear type can be configured to order.

### Advantages

#### Cleaning Performance

**Two Independent Stations**  
Both stations operate simultaneously, increasing cleaning output while keeping the machine compact for smaller brewery packaging areas.

**Pulsed Valve Cleaning**  
High- and low-pressure pulsed valves improve cleaning of keg stems and internal surfaces, supporting more reliable keg preparation.

**Steam or Chemical Sanitisation**  
Two cleaning programme options allow breweries to choose steam sterilisation or chemical sanitation depending on utilities and process preference.

#### Automation & Compatibility

**Automatic Spear Connection**  
The keg spear is locked automatically after loading, reducing operator handling and improving cycle consistency.

**Siemens PLC Control**  
Automated programmes manage the washing sequence after manual loading, reducing operator intervention during the cycle.

**10–60 L Keg Compatibility**  
Compatible with common Euro and US standard keg sizes, with spear type configured to match the customer’s keg fleet.

### Configurations

**Steam Sterilisation Programme**  
Cleaning sequence using rinse, caustic wash, hot water rinse, steam sterilisation, and CO₂ counter-pressure preparation.

**Chemical Sanitation Programme**  
Cleaning sequence using rinse, caustic wash, hot water rinse, chemical sanitisation, and CO₂ counter-pressure preparation.

**Electric Heating Configuration**  
Self-contained heating option using electric elements, suited to sites without a suitable steam supply.

**Steam Heating Configuration**  
Utility-efficient configuration for breweries with available steam, reducing electrical heating demand during keg cleaning.

## Keg Washer and Filler

The Keg Washer and Filler is a two-station, fully automatic combined washing and filling unit for breweries requiring a compact clean-and-fill solution. Kegs are loaded manually and started by double-button activation, after which the machine handles washing, sanitisation, and CO₂ counter-pressure filling in sequence.

The unit includes Siemens PLC control, a 7-inch colour HMI, dedicated caustic and sanitiser tanks, heated chemical preparation, heavy-duty stainless steel pumps, pressure monitoring, and keg level control. It is suitable for breweries needing flexible keg preparation and filling without installing a full kegging line.

### Advantages

#### Combined Washing & Filling

**Compact Two-Station Layout**  
One station washes and sanitises while the other fills, allowing two operations to run within a compact machine footprint.

**CO₂ Counter-Pressure Filling**  
Filling under counter-pressure helps protect carbonation and reduce oxygen pickup during keg packaging.

**Integrated Chemical Tanks**  
Dedicated caustic and sanitiser tanks keep cleaning chemistry available within the machine, reducing external equipment requirements.

#### Control & Operator Safety

**PLC/HMI Operation**  
The touchscreen interface manages recipes, cycle settings, alarms, and process monitoring from one operator panel.

**Double-Button Start**  
Two-hand start confirms keg positioning before the cycle begins, improving operator safety and reducing mis-seated keg starts.

**Process Monitoring**  
Digital pressure gauge, level switch, and temperature probe provide visibility and protection during washing and filling.

### Configurations

**Two-Station Washer/Filler**  
Standard compact configuration with one washing/sanitising station and one CO₂ counter-pressure filling station operating in parallel.

**Micro Matic Coupler Configuration**  
Configured for Micro Matic keg couplers, with spear type adapted to match the brewery’s keg fleet.

**Custom Keg Compatibility Configuration**  
Built for specific keg sizes and spear types where the customer operates mixed or non-standard keg inventories.

**Heated Chemical Configuration**  
Includes low-watt-density immersion heating for caustic and sanitiser tanks to maintain solution temperature during operation.
