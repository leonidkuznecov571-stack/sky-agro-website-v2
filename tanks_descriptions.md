## Beverage Tanks

Beverage tanks are general-purpose process and storage vessels used across brewing and beverage production for blending, carbonation, holding, and transfer of finished liquids. Manufactured in food-grade stainless steel, they provide a controlled, hygienic environment for handling beer, soft drinks, RTDs, and other liquid products.

Available in a wide range of capacities and configurations, beverage tanks are designed for integration into production lines, with options for insulation, cooling jackets, and pressure-rated operation where required. Their flexibility makes them suitable for both intermediate process stages and final product storage.

---

### Advantages

#### Process Flexibility & Integration

**Multi-Application Use**  
Suitable for blending, carbonation, storage, and transfer, allowing a single vessel type to serve multiple roles across production and packaging workflows.

**Scalable Capacity Options**  
Available across a broad volume range, supporting both pilot-scale operations and full commercial production with consistent design principles.

**Seamless Line Integration**  
Designed for straightforward connection to upstream and downstream equipment, including carbonation systems, fillers, and transfer lines.

---

#### Hygiene & Construction

**Sanitary Stainless Steel Build**  
Food-grade SS304 construction with smooth internal finish supports hygienic handling of sensitive beverage products and simplifies cleaning validation.

**CIP-Ready Design**  
Integrated spray devices and sanitary fittings enable full clean-in-place operation, reducing downtime and manual intervention.

**Robust Fabrication Standards**  
Precision welding and polished finishes minimise contamination risk and ensure long-term durability under continuous production use.

---

### Configurations

**Insulated Beverage Tank**  
Fully insulated tank with optional cooling jacket for temperature-sensitive products, maintaining stability during storage, blending, or carbonation.

**Pressure-Rated Beverage Tank**  
Designed for applications requiring controlled pressure, such as carbonation or CO₂ retention, ensuring safe and consistent operation.

**Mixing / Blending Tank**  
Equipped with agitation or inline mixing capability for homogeneous blending of multiple liquid streams or additives.

**Buffer / Holding Tank**  
Intermediate storage vessel used to balance flow between production stages, ensuring consistent supply to packaging or downstream processing.

## Dairy Tanks

Dairy tanks are specialised vessels designed for hygienic handling, storage, and processing of milk and dairy products. Manufactured in food-grade stainless steel, they support strict sanitation standards required for dairy applications, including temperature control and contamination prevention.

Configured for cooling, storage, or processing, dairy tanks maintain product integrity through controlled temperature management and hygienic design, ensuring compliance with food safety requirements across dairy operations.

---

### Advantages

#### Product Integrity & Temperature Control

**Efficient Cooling Performance**  
Integrated cooling jackets maintain precise temperature control, critical for preserving milk quality and preventing bacterial growth during storage.

**Stable Storage Conditions**  
Insulation ensures consistent product temperature over extended periods, supporting safe handling and transport readiness.

**Gentle Product Handling**  
Designed to minimise agitation and shear, preserving the physical and chemical integrity of dairy products.

---

#### Hygiene & Compliance

**High-Sanitation Design**  
Smooth internal surfaces and sanitary fittings minimise contamination risk and meet stringent dairy hygiene standards.

**CIP Compatibility**  
Full clean-in-place capability enables reliable and repeatable cleaning cycles without disassembly, essential for regulated environments.

**Food Safety Compliance**  
Manufactured to international standards, supporting regulatory compliance across dairy processing operations.

---

### Configurations

**Milk Storage Tank**  
Insulated tank designed for chilled storage of raw or processed milk, maintaining consistent temperature prior to further processing or distribution.

**Cooling Tank**  
Equipped with high-efficiency cooling systems for rapid temperature reduction immediately after milking or processing.

**Processing Tank**  
Configured for mixing or treatment processes, including agitation and temperature control for dairy product preparation.

**Transport / Buffer Tank**  
Used for temporary storage and transfer between processing stages, ensuring stable flow and product handling within dairy facilities.

## Serving Tanks

Serving tanks are vessels used for final storage and direct dispensing of finished beverages, typically installed in taprooms, bars, or hospitality environments. They allow beer or other drinks to be served directly from the tank, reducing handling and preserving product freshness.

Manufactured in stainless steel and available in insulated or single-wall formats, serving tanks provide a practical solution for maintaining product quality while simplifying dispense systems.

---

### Advantages

#### Dispense Efficiency & Freshness

**Direct-to-Tap Serving**  
Eliminates the need for kegs by allowing product to be dispensed directly from the tank, reducing handling and labour.

**Reduced Oxygen Exposure**  
Closed-system serving minimises oxidation, helping maintain flavour stability and product quality.

**Consistent Serving Conditions**  
Temperature and pressure control ensure stable carbonation and consistent pour quality.

---

#### Operational Simplicity

**Reduced Packaging Requirements**  
Removes reliance on kegs or intermediate packaging, lowering operational complexity and cost.

**Compact Installation Options**  
Suitable for installation in visible or back-of-house environments, including stacked or horizontal layouts where space is limited.

**Easy Cleaning & Maintenance**  
CIP-compatible design simplifies cleaning cycles, supporting hygiene without disrupting service operations.

---

### Configurations

**Single-Wall Serving Tank**  
Non-insulated tank for environments with external temperature control, typically used in bars or taprooms for short-term serving.

**Insulated Serving Tank**  
Fully insulated configuration maintaining product temperature independently, suitable for venues without controlled ambient conditions.

**Horizontal Serving Tank**  
Designed for low-height installations, often used in under-bar or cellar applications with limited vertical space.

**Stacked Serving Tank System**  
Multiple tanks configured vertically to maximise serving capacity within constrained floor areas.

## Water Tanks

Water tanks are storage and process vessels used for holding brewing and process water, including hot liquor, cold liquor, and treated water streams. Manufactured in food-grade stainless steel, they ensure clean, reliable water supply across brewing and beverage operations.

Designed for integration into utility and process systems, water tanks support temperature control, water treatment, and consistent supply to critical production stages.

---

### Advantages

#### Process Reliability & Supply

**Stable Water Availability**  
Provides consistent storage and supply of process water, ensuring uninterrupted operation across brewing or production stages.

**Integration with Treatment Systems**  
Compatible with filtration, deaeration, and dosing systems, supporting precise water preparation and control.

**Flexible Capacity Scaling**  
Available in multiple sizes to match production requirements, from small systems to large-scale installations.

---

#### Construction & Durability

**Corrosion-Resistant Materials**  
SS304 construction ensures long service life and resistance to corrosion from treated or process water.

**Temperature Management Options**  
Available with insulation and heating or cooling systems depending on application (e.g. hot liquor vs cold storage).

**Low Maintenance Design**  
Simple, robust construction reduces maintenance requirements and supports long-term operational reliability.

---

### Configurations

**Cold Water Tank**  
Storage vessel for untreated or treated cold water, supplying process stages requiring stable, ambient or chilled water.

**Hot Liquor Tank (HLT)**  
Insulated and heated tank designed for storing hot brewing water, ensuring consistent availability for mashing and cleaning operations.

**Treated Water Tank**  
Used for storing water post-treatment (e.g. filtration or chemical adjustment), maintaining quality prior to use in production.

**Buffer / Process Water Tank**  
Intermediate storage tank balancing supply and demand between water treatment systems and production processes.
