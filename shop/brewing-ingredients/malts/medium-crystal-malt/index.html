Medium Crystal Malt

Colour: 100–150 EBC

Best for: Amber Ale, Brown Ale, Porter

Medium Crystal Malt is used in ales and dark beers to provide deeper colour, rounded caramel flavour, and increased body with added complexity.

SKY Medium Crystal Malt develops a deeper amber colour and a more pronounced flavour profile, with notes of caramel, toasted bread, and light nuttiness. The caramelisation process contributes to a smoother, more rounded palate.

It is particularly effective in building mid-palate depth and complexity. Used in balanced proportions, it enhances structure and richness without overpowering other ingredients.
