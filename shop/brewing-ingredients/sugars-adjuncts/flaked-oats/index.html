Flaked Oats

Texture, Haze & Mouthfeel Builder

Flaked Oats are a pre-gelatinised cereal adjunct used to build a smoother, creamier palate and fuller mouthfeel in beer. Popular in hazy IPAs, oatmeal stouts and speciality ales, they contribute body and haze potential without the need for cereal cooking.

Flaked Oats are oat grains that have been steamed and rolled, making their starches accessible to mash enzymes without a separate cereal-cooking stage. In brewing they are valued less for extract contribution and more for the way they influence texture, head retention and visual appearance. Their beta-glucans and proteins can help create a soft, rounded palate and a stable haze when used alongside suitable base malt, yeast and hopping regimes.

They are widely used in hazy pale ales and New England IPAs, where they support a full mouthfeel and opaque appearance without pushing the beer towards heavy sweetness. Flaked Oats are also a traditional choice in oatmeal stouts, adding silkiness and depth to dark malt character, and can be used in porters, milds and speciality beers where extra palate weight is desired.

Typical usage varies by style and process. Rates of 5-20% of the grist are common, with higher rates possible where the recipe is designed around a very soft texture or pronounced haze. Because oats contain no husk and can increase mash viscosity, brewers should consider mash tun loading, lautering performance and the use of rice hulls where higher inclusion rates are planned.

Flaked Oats are suitable for:

  • Building soft, creamy mouthfeel in hazy IPAs and pale ales
  • Supporting stable haze in modern hop-forward beers
  • Adding silkiness and body to oatmeal stouts
  • Increasing palate weight in porters, milds and speciality ales
  • Reducing the need for cereal cooking in adjunct-heavy recipes
  • Recipes where a fuller texture is needed without strong malt flavour
