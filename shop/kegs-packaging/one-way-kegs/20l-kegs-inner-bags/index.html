20L keg with bag

Capacity: 20 litres

The 20L keg with bag features a built-in sterile aluminium foil inner bag and inner bag compression dispensing system, eliminating the need for food-grade CO2 and associated gas handling equipment. It delivers oxygen-free, fresh-keeping beer dispense with an automatic pressure relief valve for transport and dispense safety.

The 20L keg with bag is a single-use explosion-proof keg built around an inner bag compression dispensing system. A sterile aluminium foil inner bag is contained within the double-layer PET barrel structure. During dispense, CO2 or compressed air enters the space between the PET container and the inner bag through the keg coupler, squeezing the bag to push beer out without the gas ever contacting the beer itself. This keeps the beer fully oxygen-free and preserves flavour and freshness throughout the keg's life.

Because beer is isolated from the pressurising gas by the inner bag, there is no requirement for food-grade CO2, gas cylinders, pressure regulators, or the associated equipment and transportation costs. The 20L format is well suited to accounts with moderate volume requirements, event use, and any outlet where the 30L format represents excessive batch size. An automatic pressure relief valve is built into the closure to ensure safety during transport and at the point of dispense.

The keg is compatible with standard keg couplers across types A, D, S, M, F, U, G, and T, and with standard filling equipment, giving breweries and distributors full flexibility across their existing dispense infrastructure.

The 20L keg with bag is suitable for:

  • Brewery-conditioned beer for on-trade, event, and smaller-account dispense
  • Operations seeking to eliminate food-grade CO2 and gas cylinder costs
  • Accounts where a 30L volume is excessive
  • Use with standard keg couplers (A/D/S/M/F/U/G/T types) and standard filling equipment
