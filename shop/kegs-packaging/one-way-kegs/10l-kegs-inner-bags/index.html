10L keg with bag

Capacity: 10 litres

The 10L keg with bag features a built-in sterile aluminium foil inner bag and inner bag compression dispensing system, eliminating the need for food-grade CO2 and gas handling equipment. It delivers oxygen-free, fresh-keeping dispense in a compact format ideal for smaller accounts, tasting events, and hospitality use.

The 10L keg with bag is a single-use explosion-proof keg built around an inner bag compression dispensing system. A sterile aluminium foil inner bag is contained within the double-layer PET barrel structure. During dispense, CO2 or compressed air enters the space between the PET container and the inner bag through the keg coupler, squeezing the bag to push beer out without the gas ever contacting the beer itself. This keeps the beer fully oxygen-free and preserves flavour and freshness throughout the keg's life.

The 10L format is particularly well suited to smaller on-trade accounts, tasting rooms, events, pop-ups, and hospitality settings where volume requirements are modest or where multiple products need to be offered simultaneously without the commitment of a larger keg. No food-grade CO2, gas cylinders, pressure regulators, or related equipment costs are required. The keg is compatible with standard keg couplers across types A, D, S, M, F, U, G, and T, and an automatic pressure relief valve ensures safety during transport and dispense.

The 10L keg with bag is suitable for:

  • Smaller on-trade accounts, tasting rooms, and hospitality settings
  • Events, pop-ups, and festival dispense where modest volumes are required
  • Offering a wider range of products on tap without large volume commitments
  • Operations seeking to eliminate food-grade CO2 and gas cylinder costs
