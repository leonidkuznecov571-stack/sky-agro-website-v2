10L keg without bag

Capacity: 10 litres

The 10L keg without bag operates identically to a traditional steel keg, with no recycling or return required. Built from high-barrier, explosion-proof, UV-filtering brown PET with no internal cleaning needed, it offers high cost performance in a compact format for smaller accounts and event dispense.

The 10L keg without bag is a single-use explosion-proof keg designed to match the structure, filling process, and usage method of conventional steel kegs, allowing seamless integration with existing brewery filling lines and dispense infrastructure. CO2 enters the keg through the keg coupler, beer enters from the bottom of the spear tube under pressure, and dispense is completed through the keg coupler — identical in operation to a standard returnable steel keg.

The keg body is manufactured from a high-barrier, explosion-proof PET material with enhanced pressure resistance. The brown PET construction filters UV rays to protect beer quality and flavour during storage and transit, and the high-barrier material prevents gas exchange and CO2 escape to maintain consistent carbonation and shelf life.

The 10L format is ideal for smaller on-trade accounts, tasting events, hospitality, and any application where a full-size keg would result in significant wastage or where multiple different products are required simultaneously. As a single-use keg there is no return, cleaning, or re-certification requirement, and the interior requires no cleaning prior to filling.

The 10L keg without bag is suitable for:

  • Smaller on-trade accounts, tasting rooms, events, and hospitality settings
  • Offering a wider range of products on tap at lower volume commitments
  • Operations reducing or eliminating steel keg return logistics
  • UV-sensitive beer styles requiring light protection during storage and transit
