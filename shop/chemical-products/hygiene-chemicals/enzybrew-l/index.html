Enzybrew L

Enzybrew L is a professional-use liquid enzymatic detergent designed specifically for cleaning bottling machines and soaking removable components - including valves, rubber hoses, and fittings - in microbrewery environments. With a phosphorus-free formula, it provides a thorough and environmentally responsible cleaning solution that targets the organic soiling typically encountered in brewing operations.

This product works by harnessing the action of enzymes - including subtilisin - to break down protein waste, polysaccharide residues, and other organic deposits that accumulate on contact surfaces and removable parts. The result is reinforced hygiene, effective preparation for disinfection, and a guaranteed absence of contamination risk. Because the product is non-corrosive and non-classified for hazard under normal use conditions, it presents no risk of burns to operators and is safe for use with soft metals and delicate equipment (testing on a small surface first is recommended for prolonged contact with aluminium, iron, or copper).

Enzybrew L is applied as a 1% solution in hot water for soaking removable parts for 4 to 12 hours, or as a CIP cleaning solution for 20 to 60 minutes. Its non-foaming formula makes rinsing fast and straightforward. With a biodegradation rate exceeding 99% in 28 days, it is a sustainable alternative to caustic soda-based cleaners.

Enzybrew L is suitable for:

  • Soaking removable parts (valves, rubber hoses, fittings, and similar components)
  • CIP cleaning of bottling machines and associated pipework
  • Phosphorus-free and environmentally responsible cleaning programmes
  • Use as an alternative to caustic soda cleaners
  • Microbrewery bottling line hygiene

Enzybrew L is not classified as hazardous under CLP Regulation (EC) No. 1272/2008. It contains subtilisin and a trace preservative (methylisothiazolinone reaction mass) that may produce an allergic reaction in sensitised individuals.

  • Not classified as hazardous under CLP regulations
  • Contains subtilisin and preservative - may produce an allergic reaction (EUH208)
  • May cause mild skin and eye irritation on direct contact

Safety precautions:

  • Wear appropriate eye protection and insulated gloves during handling
  • Do not breathe vapours - use in a well-ventilated area or with local exhaust ventilation
  • Do not eat, drink, or smoke when using this product
  • Wash hands and exposed areas thoroughly after handling

Handling and Storage:

  • Store in the original closed container at 4-25 C in a cool, well-ventilated place
  • Keep away from direct sunlight and heat sources
  • Use within 12 months of the date of manufacture
  • Dispose of as a detergent containing dangerous substances in accordance with local regulations
