Enzyfoam is a self-foaming liquid enzymatic detergent formulated for open plant cleaning across the food industry, including breweries and other food production environments. It is specially developed for use on any open surface - floors, walls, drains, spiral freezers, conveyor systems, and difficult-to-access or high-risk areas - where heavily encrusted organic residues present a hygiene challenge.

This product works through an enzymatic cocktail that actively breaks down and removes proteins, grease, and starch that have become encrusted on surfaces, providing a depth of cleaning that conventional chemical products cannot achieve. Applied with a foaming dispenser, the foam clings to vertical surfaces and difficult-to-reach areas, extending contact time and reducing the need for manual scrubbing. Once applied, the foam is allowed to act for 15 to 30 minutes before rinsing - a simple, efficient process that also prevents clogging in drains and pipes through the continuous action of enzymes in the waste stream.

Enzyfoam has a neutral pH in solution (6.9-8.9) and produces no dangerous fumes, making it safe for operators without risk of chemical burns and safe for use on soft metals and delicate equipment. With a biodegradability rate exceeding 95% in 28 days (OECD 302B), it supports responsible waste water discharge and sustainable operations.

Enzyfoam is suitable for:

  • Open surface cleaning of floors, walls, and drains
  • Spiral freezers and conveyor systems
  • Soft-metal and sensitive food production equipment
  • Food-contact surface hygiene programmes
  • High-risk and difficult-to-access areas in food production environments
  • Drain and pipe maintenance through enzymatic action

Enzyfoam (pure product) is classified under GHS/CLP as a skin irritant (Category 2, H315) and a serious eye hazard (Category 1, H318). When used at the recommended concentration of 1%, it is not classified as hazardous. It contains subtilisin, which may cause respiratory sensitisation on prolonged inhalation of mist or aerosol.

Pure product hazards:

  • Causes skin irritation (H315)
  • Causes serious eye damage (H318)
  • Contains subtilisin - may cause allergy or asthma symptoms if inhaled (H334)

At recommended use concentration (1%):

  • Not classified as hazardous

Safety precautions:

  • Wear chemical-resistant gloves and safety glasses or chemical goggles
  • If mist or aerosol is generated, use an approved respirator with P3 filter
  • Do not eat, drink, or smoke when using this product
  • Wash hands and exposed areas thoroughly after handling
  • Emergency eye wash fountains and safety showers should be available nearby

Handling and Storage:

  • Store in the original closed container at 4-25 C
  • Keep away from direct sunlight and heat sources
  • Use within 12 months of production date, and within 6 months of opening
  • Prevent entry to sewers and public waters in the event of a spill
  • Dispose of contents in accordance with local regulations
