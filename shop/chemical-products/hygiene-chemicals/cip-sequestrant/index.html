CIP Sequestrant is a placeholder product page for SKY LABS hygiene chemical supply. It is intended for use as a supporting additive in brewery and beverage cleaning programmes where mineral hardness can interfere with alkaline cleaning performance.

The product supports caustic CIP detergents by binding hardness salts and helping keep soil and mineral residues in suspension during cleaning. Final technical copy, dosage guidance and document downloads are to be confirmed.

CIP Sequestrant is expected to be suitable for:

  • Brewery CIP cleaning programmes
  • Use alongside caustic detergents in hard water areas
  • Fermenters, bright beer tanks and process pipework
  • Food and beverage processing environments

Safety classification and handling guidance are to be confirmed against the final SDS.

  • Refer to the product SDS before use
  • Wear suitable protective equipment when handling chemical products
  • Use only as part of a validated cleaning procedure
  • Store in the original container with the closure secured
