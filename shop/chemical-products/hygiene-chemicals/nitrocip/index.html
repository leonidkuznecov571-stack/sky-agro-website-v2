Nitrocip (Nitrocip Acid Descaler) is a highly effective acidic detergent developed for descaling and cleaning applications in the food and beverage industry. Nitrocip Acid Descaler is specifically designed for use in CIP (Clean-in-Place) systems, ensuring efficient removal of mineral scale, inorganic deposits, and process residues.

Based on a combination of nitric acid and phosphoric acid, Nitrocip delivers powerful cleaning performance while maintaining compatibility with industrial processing equipment. It is suitable for use in breweries, dairy plants, and other food production facilities where hygiene and efficiency are critical.

Nitrocip is suitable for general industrial descaling where acid-based cleaning is required, and is recommended for:

  • CIP cleaning systems
  • Brewing equipment and tanks
  • Dairy processing installations
  • Food production lines
  • Heat exchangers
  • Pipework and process vessels

Nitrocip is classified as a hazardous chemical and must be handled with care.

  • Causes severe skin burns and eye damage (H314)
  • Corrosive product

For full information, please refer to SDS.

Safety precautions:

  • Do not breathe vapour or spray
  • Wear protective gloves, protective clothing, and eye/face protection
  • Use only in well-ventilated areas
  • Avoid contact with skin and eyes
  • In case of exposure, follow first aid instructions and seek medical attention immediately.

Handling and Storage:

  • Store in a cool, well-ventilated place
  • Keep container tightly closed
  • Store in original container with vented cap
  • Avoid exposure to incompatible materials
  • Use appropriate protective equipment during handling
