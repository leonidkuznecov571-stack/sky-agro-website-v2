Caustic Super is a high-performance sodium hydroxide-based alkaline cleaner developed for cleaning applications in the food and beverage industry. The product is specifically designed for use in CIP (Clean-in-Place) systems, ensuring effective removal of organic residues, fats, and protein-based contamination from processing equipment.

This alkaline CIP detergent is suitable for use in breweries, dairy processing facilities, and general food production plants. It can be applied across a wide range of equipment and is effective at both ambient and elevated temperatures up to 80°C, ensuring consistent and efficient cleaning performance.

Formulated with sodium hydroxide and chelating agents, Caustic Super delivers reliable cleaning performance in demanding industrial environments, including areas with exceptionally hard water. The product is particularly effective in brewery cleaning applications, including cask washing and control of beer stone deposits.

Caustic Super is designed for effective removal of fats, proteins, and organic residues, and is suitable for use in:

  • CIP cleaning systems
  • Brewing equipment, vessels, and tanks
  • Cask washing operations
  • Dairy processing installations
  • Food production lines
  • Pipework and process systems

Caustic Super is a hazardous alkaline product and must be handled with care.

  • Causes severe skin burns and eye damage (H314)
  • Corrosive product

For full information, please refer to SDS.

Safety precautions:

  • Do not breathe vapour or spray
  • Wear protective gloves, protective clothing, and eye/face protection
  • Use only in well-ventilated areas
  • Avoid contact with skin and eyes In case of exposure, follow first aid instructions and seek medical attention immediately.

Handling and Storage:

  • Store in a cool, well-ventilated place
  • Keep container tightly closed
  • Store in original container
  • Keep away from acids
  • Avoid direct sunlight and freezing
  • Use appropriate protective equipment during handling
