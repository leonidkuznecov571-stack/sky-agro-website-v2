Microcid SB is a brewery-grade antimicrobial and antioxidant stabiliser designed for use in finished beer. Based on a synergistic blend of potassium sorbate and sulphite compounds, it protects against yeast and bacterial spoilage while preventing oxidative degradation, helping to preserve the intended flavour and stability of the final product.

Its targeted action inhibits unwanted refermentation and extends shelf life without impacting sensory quality when used at recommended dosages. Applied at the bright beer stage or prior to packaging, Microcid SB provides a reliable solution for maintaining microbiological control and product consistency in both filtered and unfiltered beers.

Microcid SB is designed to provide microbiological stability and oxidative protection in finished beer, and is suitable for use in:

  • Bright beer tanks (BBT)
  • Inline dosing prior to packaging
  • Filtered and unfiltered beer (with controlled yeast counts)
  • Prevention of refermentation in packaged beer
  • Shelf-life extension through antimicrobial and antioxidant action

For full technical and safety guidance, please refer to the Technical Data Sheet and Safety Data Sheet.

Microcid SB is a hazardous product and must be handled with care.

  • Causes serious eye damage (H318)
  • May cause respiratory irritation (H335)
  • Contact with acids liberates toxic gas (EUH031)

For full information, please refer to SDS.

Safety precautions:

  • Avoid breathing dust
  • Wear protective gloves, protective clothing, and eye/face protection
  • Use only in well-ventilated areas
  • Avoid contact with eyes and prolonged exposure
  • In case of exposure, follow first aid instructions and seek medical attention if required

Handling and Storage

  • Store in a cool, dry, well-ventilated place
  • Keep container tightly closed and in original packaging
  • Protect from heat sources and direct sunlight
  • Avoid contact with incompatible materials such as acids
  • Use appropriate protective equipment during handling
