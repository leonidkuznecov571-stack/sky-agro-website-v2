Calcium Chloride Flake is a versatile inorganic salt widely used in industrial, food, and water treatment applications. The product is supplied in flake or granular form and is highly soluble in water, allowing rapid and efficient dosing in various processes.

This product is commonly used for water hardness adjustment, mineral supplementation in food and beverage production, and moisture control in industrial environments. Due to its hygroscopic properties, calcium chloride effectively absorbs moisture and helps maintain optimal process conditions.

Calcium Chloride Flake is suitable for use in food processing, brewing, and general industrial operations where reliable performance and consistent quality are required. It provides stable performance across a wide range of applications and is easy to handle under appropriate storage conditions.

Calcium Chloride Flake is designed for water conditioning, mineral control, and moisture management, and is suitable for:

  • Water treatment and hardness adjustment
  • Brewing and beverage production
  • Food processing applications
  • Moisture control in industrial environments
  • General industrial processes
  • Mineral supplementation

Calcium Chloride Flake is a hazardous material and must be handled with care.

  • Causes serious eye irritation (H319)
  • May cause irritation to skin and respiratory system

For full information, please refer to SDS.

Safety precautions:

  • Wear protective gloves, protective clothing, and eye/face protection
  • Avoid contact with skin and eyes
  • Ensure adequate ventilation during handling
  • Avoid inhalation of dust
  • In case of exposure, rinse thoroughly with water and seek medical attention if irritation persists.

Handling and Storage:

  • Store in a dry, cool, well-ventilated place
  • Keep container tightly closed
  • Store in original container
  • Keep away from incompatible materials (e.g. chlorines and strong bases)
  • Avoid direct sunlight and freezing
  • Use appropriate protective equipment during handling
