Mashtreat is a specialised brewing water treatment additive developed to optimise mineral composition during the mashing process. The product is designed to enhance water chemistry, improving enzyme activity and overall brewing efficiency.

Formulated as a blend of calcium sulphate and calcium chloride, Mashtreat increases calcium levels while introducing balanced sulphate and chloride ions into the brewing liquor. This supports improved mash conversion, enhances wort clarity, and contributes to better extract yield and fermentability.

Mashtreat also promotes yeast health and vigour, improves protein precipitation during wort boiling, and supports overall beer stability. The balanced mineral profile helps refine flavour characteristics, contributing to improved mouthfeel, bitterness balance, and head retention.

The product is suitable for use in breweries where precise control of water composition is essential for achieving consistent product quality. It is typically added to the mash or grist to ensure optimal distribution of minerals throughout the brewing process.

Mashtreat is designed for mineral optimisation and mash performance improvement, and is suited for:

  • Mash water treatment
  • Brewing water mineral adjustment
  • Wort production processes
  • Breweries and craft brewing operations
  • Beverage production

Mashtreat is a hazardous product and must be handled with care.

  • Causes serious eye irritation (H319)
  • May cause irritation to skin and respiratory system

Safety precautions:

  • Wear protective gloves, protective clothing, and eye protection
  • Avoid contact with eyes and skin
  • Ensure adequate ventilation during handling
  • Avoid inhalation of dust
  • In case of exposure, rinse thoroughly with water and seek medical attention if irritation persists.

Handling and Storage:

  • Store in a cool, well-ventilated place
  • Keep container tightly closed
  • Store in original container
  • Protect from moisture and humidity
  • Avoid direct sunlight and freezing
  • Use appropriate protective equipment during handling
