Water Tanks

Capacity range: 1–5,000 hL

Water tanks are storage and process vessels used for holding brewing and process water, including hot liquor, cold liquor, and treated water streams. Manufactured in food-grade stainless steel, they ensure clean, reliable water supply across brewing and beverage operations.

Designed for integration into utility and process systems, water tanks support temperature control, water treatment, and consistent supply to critical production stages.

Construction & Durability

  1. 04

    Corrosion-Resistant Materials

    SS304 construction ensures long service life and resistance to corrosion from treated or process water.

  2. 05

    Temperature Management Options

    Available with insulation and heating or cooling systems depending on application (e.g. hot liquor vs cold storage).

  3. 06

    Low Maintenance Design

    Simple, robust construction reduces maintenance requirements and supports long-term operational reliability.

Four available configurations

  1. Cold Water Tank

    Storage vessel for untreated or treated cold water, supplying process stages requiring stable, ambient or chilled water.

  2. Hot Liquor Tank (HLT)

    Insulated and heated tank designed for storing hot brewing water, ensuring consistent availability for mashing and cleaning operations.

  3. Treated Water Tank

    Used for storing water post-treatment (e.g. filtration or chemical adjustment), maintaining quality prior to use in production.

  4. Buffer / Process Water Tank

    Intermediate storage tank balancing supply and demand between water treatment systems and production processes.
