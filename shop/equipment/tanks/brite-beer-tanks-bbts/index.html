Brite Beer Tanks

Capacity: 500–20,000 L

Brite beer tanks are conditioning vessels used for final clarification, carbonation, and storage prior to packaging. Manufactured in food-grade SS304 stainless steel, they deliver a controlled environment for stabilising finished beer and preparing it for consistent, oxygen-minimised transfer to kegs, cans, or bottles.

Available from 1 hL to 500 hL, BBTs are fully insulated and dimple-jacketed for glycol cooling, ensuring stable temperature throughout conditioning and carbonation. Pressure-rated construction and sanitary fittings support safe operation, reliable CO₂ retention, and repeatable product quality across production scales.

Process Control & Product Stability

  1. 01

    Precise Temperature Control

    Dimple-jacketed cooling and full insulation maintain stable conditioning temperatures, allowing controlled carbonation and minimising variation between batches, particularly critical for bright beer consistency prior to packaging.

  2. 02

    Pressure Integrity & CO₂ Retention

    Pressure-rated vessel design supports carbonation under controlled conditions, ensuring consistent dissolved CO₂ levels and reducing losses during storage and transfer.

  3. 03

    Consistent Final Product Quality

    Controlled environment limits oxygen pickup and temperature fluctuation, preserving flavour, carbonation, and clarity from conditioning through to packaging.

Hygiene & Operational Reliability

  1. 04

    Full CIP Compatibility

    Integrated spray ball, CIP arm, and sanitary fittings ensure complete internal coverage, enabling repeatable cleaning cycles without disassembly and reducing contamination risk.

  2. 05

    Sanitary Construction Standards

    SS304 construction with smooth internal finish (Ra ≤ 0.6 μm) and polished welds minimises product hold-up and supports hygienic operation across all process stages.

  3. 06

    Integrated Monitoring & Sampling

    Thermowells, sample valves, and pressure instrumentation allow in-process checks without compromising vessel integrity, supporting quality control and operational visibility.

Four available configurations

  1. Vertical Brite Beer Tank

    Standard configuration for most breweries. Insulated and jacketed, suitable for conditioning, carbonation, and direct serving in controlled cellar environments with efficient footprint and straightforward integration.

  2. Horizontal Brite Beer Tank

    Designed for low-headroom installations or constrained layouts. Maintains identical specification to vertical tanks while optimising space utilisation where ceiling height is limited.

  3. Stacked Brite Beer Tank System

    Stackable configuration enabling multiple tanks to be installed vertically, maximising conditioning capacity within limited floor space without requiring building expansion.

  4. Single-Wall Serving Tank

    Non-insulated configuration intended for short-term serving applications, typically in taprooms or bars where ambient temperature is externally controlled rather than via integrated cooling.
