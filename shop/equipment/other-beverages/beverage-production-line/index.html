Beverage Production Line

Ingredient Streams: Up to 4 + water

The Beverage Production Line is an integrated inline processing system for high-flexibility beverage production. It combines proportional blending, temperature control, carbonation, optional pasteurisation, and dispensing or buffer tank management within a continuous process.

Deaerated water and up to four additional ingredient streams, including syrup, flavour, wine, spirits, or other liquids, are dosed into a static mixing unit according to HMI-selected recipes. The line is suited to RTDs, soft drinks, alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages, and carbonated products requiring repeatable blending with minimal product loss.

Multi-Stage Production

  1. 01

    Integrated Processing

    Blending, carbonation, temperature control, pasteurisation, and tank management can be combined into one continuous line, reducing transfer steps and product handling.

  2. 02

    Multi-Stream Blending

    Up to four ingredient streams plus deaerated water can be metered into the system, supporting a wide range of recipes and beverage formats.

  3. 03

    Batch or Continuous Operation

    The line can support both batch and continuous production modes, giving producers flexibility across product types and production volumes.

Control & Product Consistency

  1. 04

    Recipe-Based HMI Operation

    Operators select recipes through the HMI, with ingredient dosing, carbonation, and tank routing controlled according to programmed parameters.

  2. 05

    Automated Tank Switching

    Buffer and storage tanks can be managed so that one tank supplies the filler while another receives blended product, reducing process interruptions.

  3. 06

    Low Product Loss

    Inline dosing and controlled tank management reduce unnecessary transfers, helping preserve product volume and quality during changeovers.

Four available configurations

  1. Blending and Carbonation Line

    Core line combining multi-stream proportional blending with inline CO₂ or N₂ dosing for carbonated beverage production.

  2. Blending, Carbonation and Pasteurisation Line

    Integrated configuration adding flash pasteurisation for products requiring microbial control prior to filling.

  3. RTD Beverage Production Line

    Recipe-led system for alcoholic and non-alcoholic ready-to-drink products using multiple liquid streams and controlled carbonation.

  4. Custom Beverage Plant

    Full production-line configuration specified around ingredient streams, pasteurisation needs, tank volumes, filling equipment, automation level, and site layout.
