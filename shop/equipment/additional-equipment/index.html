Additional Equipment

Supporting brewery equipment for material handling, yeast management and practical process-side operations across brewhouse and cellar.

Four additional equipment types available

  • Malt mills for controlled crushing of malt, ensuring consistent grist composition and lautering performance.
  • Tubular conveyors for enclosed, efficient transport of malt and dry materials across brewhouse and storage areas.
  • Yeast propagation systems for controlled, sterile yeast growth, supporting consistent pitching and fermentation.
  • Dry hopping guns for closed, oxygen-minimised hop dosing into fermentation and conditioning vessels.
