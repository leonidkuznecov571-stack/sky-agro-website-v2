Dry Hopping Gun

The Dry-Hopping Gun is a pressurised dosing device for introducing hops into fermentation or conditioning vessels without oxygen ingress. It is designed to preserve product integrity during dry hopping while maintaining closed-system operation.

The unit connects directly to tanks and uses CO₂ pressure to transfer hops into the vessel, minimising exposure to air and reducing the risk of oxidation.
