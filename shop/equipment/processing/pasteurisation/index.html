Pasteurisation

Batch and inline pasteurisation systems for stabilising packaged or process product while maintaining line efficiency.

Two configurations available

  • Cabinet pasteurisers for batch thermal treatment of packaged product in controlled cycles.
  • Flash pasteurisers for inline thermal treatment integrated into continuous process flow.
