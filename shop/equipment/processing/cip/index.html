CIP Equipment

Clean-in-place systems for brewery vessels and pipework, available as mobile carts or skid-mounted stations. No disassembly required.

Two CIP systems available

  • CIP Cart for flexible, mobile cleaning of single vessels and cellar equipment.
  • Auto CIP Station for automated multi-vessel cleaning with programmable cycles and centralised control.
