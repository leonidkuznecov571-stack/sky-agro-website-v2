Oxygen control

Deaeration System

Deaerated water is critical in modern brewing where oxygen control directly impacts flavour stability and shelf life. Produced via a CO₂ stripping packed tower, the system removes dissolved oxygen to below 0.02 ppm, delivering consistently low-oxygen water for dilution, high-gravity brewing, and packaging applications. Automated flow and CO₂ dosing ensure stable, repeatable performance under varying process conditions.

The system integrates a stainless steel packed tower, controlled CO₂ aeration, and PLC-driven process control to maintain precise operating parameters. Pre-fabricated pipework and hygienic construction support straightforward installation and reliable long-term operation, while integrated CIP functionality ensures consistent performance without fouling or loss of efficiency.

Precision Oxygen Removal

  1. 01

    Low Dissolved Oxygen Levels

    Efficient CO₂ stripping within a packed tower reduces dissolved oxygen to below 0.02 ppm, ensuring water quality suitable for oxygen-sensitive processes. This directly supports improved flavour stability, reduced oxidation risk, and extended shelf life across packaged beer and beverages.

  2. 02

    Stable, Automated Operation

    PLC and HMI control continuously regulate water flow, CO₂ dosing, and system levels, maintaining optimal deaeration performance without manual intervention. Real-time monitoring and adjustment ensure consistent output quality, even under changing process conditions or varying production demand.

  3. 03

    Hygienic Design with Integrated CIP

    Constructed in SS304 with fully integrated CIP pipework, the system maintains sanitary conditions across all wetted surfaces. Automated cleaning cycles prevent scaling and biofilm formation within the tower and pipework, preserving performance efficiency and ensuring compliance with brewing hygiene standards.
